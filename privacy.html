Last updated: January 2026

Introduction

InsightHub (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

Third-Party Services

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our forms. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us via the Contact page to make a request.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact
