Overview & Performance

The Maybelline Lash Sensational Sky High Mascara has taken the beauty world by storm, and for good reason. Designed to deliver full volume and limitless length from every angle, it features an exclusive flex tower brush that bends to volumize and extend every single lash from root to tip.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Exceptional length and definition
  • Flex tower brush reaches tiny corner lashes
  • Lightweight formula doesn't weigh lashes down
  • Affordable drugstore price point

Cons

  • Can clump slightly if heavily layered
  • Washable version is not waterproof

Application Tips

For the best results with Maybelline Sky High, start with clean, dry lashes and wipe any excess mascara off the wand to prevent clumping. Place the wand at the roots of your upper lashes and gently wiggle it back and forth before slowly pulling it upward toward the tips. As you move upward, slightly angle the wand outward to create more length and lift. Use the tip of the wand to reach the smaller lashes in the inner and outer corners. For extra volume, focus on the roots, and for extra length, concentrate on slowly pulling the product all the way to the lash tips. Let the first coat dry slightly before applying a second thin coat, rather than applying one thick layer. For the lower lashes, use just the tip of the wand and apply a small amount to avoid smudging. Avoid pumping the wand repeatedly into the tube, since this can introduce air and dry out the mascara faster. For the most natural, long, separated look, one to two thin coats usually work best.

The Verdict

If you are looking for an everyday mascara that provides dramatic length without flaking or feeling heavy, the Maybelline Sky High Mascara is an absolute must-have addition to your makeup bag. Its performance rivals luxury brands at a fraction of the cost.
